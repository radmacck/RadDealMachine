# Seller playbook provenance and access notes

This is internal provenance for guidance already shown in the existing call brief, not an operator reading list or a substitute for system research. Guidance is independently synthesized; it does not copy substantial course material or claim to describe an individual seller. Access date for the new references: 2026-10-10. Publication dates were not displayed on the pages reviewed.

## Public material used in the current operational entries

- **Jerry Norton / Flipping Mastery**, “5 Tips For Getting Bank Owned REOs Cheap,” https://flippingmastery.com/5_tips_for_getting_bank_owned_reos. Public article page fetched without sign-in or payment. The page attributes the article to Jerry Norton through the site, but no byline or publication date was displayed. Topic: U.S. bank-owned REO offers and repair-cost context; no state-specific rule was established. The source claims that documented repair costs can help explain an offer and describes REO preferences around offer certainty. The operational entry treats these only as educator claims, limits them to REO context, and explicitly rejects waiving inspection/raising EMD/dual-agency as automatic tactics.
- **Pace Morby**, “Texting Script,” https://resources.pacejmorby.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Texting-Script.pdf. One-page PDF linked directly from Pace Morby's official public freebies catalog; fetched on 2026-10-10 without login, payment, or form submission. No PDF byline or publication date was displayed. The PDF metadata creation date is 2023-12-08; that technical metadata is not treated as a publication date. The playbook paraphrases the sequence of confirming context, inviting discussion, then asking seller price/timing questions and arranging follow-up. It does not reproduce the script's wording or treat the example as proof of efficacy or contact permission.
- **Program on Negotiation at Harvard Law School**, “Negotiation Skills for Win-Win Negotiations,” https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/listening-skills-for-maximum-success/ (retrieved 2026-10-09). Used only for general active-listening context.
- **Program on Negotiation at Harvard Law School**, “Negotiation Questions: How Asking Better Questions Leads to Better Deals,” https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/ask-better-questions-in-negotiation-nb/ (retrieved 2026-10-09). Used only for general, non-presumptive question-writing context.
