---
name: Human decision boundaries
description: Human-only decisions and the permitted scope of Radical Deal OS automation
---

Automation may prepare research, briefs, calculations, drafts, checklists, reminders, evidence checks, matching, and follow-up queues when permissions allow. A human makes calls; optionally approves and sends messages; approves offer terms and other consequential actions; and adds or approves special contract clauses. No worker or UI shortcut may bypass a required approval. Blocked actions must state why, and completion must be based on persisted evidence rather than a click or editable checkbox.

**Why:** The user explicitly defined this as the intended human loop for Block 74.

**How to apply:** Keep preparation and suggestions separate from external actions and approvals. Verify role, current version/authorization, source evidence, and actual event receipts at every consequential transition. Do not show inert controls or false completion.
