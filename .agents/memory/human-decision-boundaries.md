---
name: Human decision boundaries
description: Human-only decisions and the permitted scope of Radical Deal OS automation
---

Automation may prepare research, briefs, calculations, drafts, checklists, reminders, evidence checks, matching, and follow-up queues when permissions allow. Calls are optional, never mandatory transitions: preserve an end-to-end text-only and in-platform messaging path. Treat calls, platform messages, and SMS as distinct channels with explicit preferences and permissions; respect consent scope, opt-outs, privacy, and state-specific rules. Record an attempt, delivery, or success only from actual evidence and use SMS only through a legitimate connected provider. A human approves consequential actions and terms and reviews clauses. No worker or UI shortcut may bypass a required approval. Blocked actions must state why, and completion must be based on persisted evidence rather than a click or editable checkbox.

After the existing deal workflow reaches its verified threshold and before public/licensed marketplace release, prioritize a private founder-only workspace. It supports either buyer-criteria-to-property discovery or property-to-buyer matching, with source-backed facts and explicit uncertainty; matches are recommendations, not guarantees or contracts. Keep an app-owned stable internal user ID, an external RadID reference slot, and separate company-level and per-user Naelo interfaces as unconnected placeholders only.

**Why:** The user explicitly set optional, permissioned communication and private founder mode before public marketplace release as governing product requirements.

**How to apply:** Keep preparation and suggestions separate from external actions and approvals. Verify role, current version/authorization, source evidence, and actual event receipts at every consequential transition. Do not show inert controls or false completion. Do not start founder-mode implementation before the current deal-workflow gate passes; do not make calls required for workflow completion or claim unconnected integrations are live.
