---
name: Buyer verification boundary
description: Buyer discovery and self-reported criteria must not be treated as verified current buying demand.
---

Buyer discovery evidence, public ownership/history, and buyer-submitted profile updates establish neither verified identity nor verified current buying intent. A buyer's bearer-link self-report is **INDICATED** evidence only; it does not verify the responder, grant contact permission, provide funds evidence, or make the buyer eligible for matching. Current matching approval requires owner-reviewed, dated identity, current-buying, criteria, and funding evidence.

**Why:** The founder requires an active, vetted buyer network and explicitly rejects invented intent or approval from a profile response alone.

**How to apply:** Keep discovered prospects separate from approved buyers. Any changed or self-reported criteria must block or invalidate matching until the required evidence is reviewed; never mark outreach delivered without provider evidence.
