---
name: Buyer verification boundary
description: Buyer discovery and self-reported criteria must not be treated as verified current buying demand.
---

Buyer discovery evidence, public ownership/history, and buyer-submitted profile updates establish neither verified identity nor verified current buying intent. A buyer's bearer-link self-report is **INDICATED** evidence only; it does not verify the responder, grant contact permission, provide funds evidence, or make the buyer eligible for matching. Current matching approval requires owner-reviewed, dated identity, current-buying, criteria, and funding evidence.

**Why:** The founder requires an active, vetted buyer network and explicitly rejects invented intent or approval from a profile response alone.

**How to apply:** Keep discovered prospects separate from approved buyers. Any changed or self-reported criteria must block or invalidate matching until the required evidence is reviewed; never mark outreach delivered without provider evidence.

Exact buyer-message approval must bind the recipient, channel, exact text, and immutable packet version/snapshot; changed terms or stale permission invalidate it. Provider acceptance is not delivery, and delivery or buyer interest is never a commitment. With no connected provider, keep outbound status `NOT_SENT`.

**Why:** The user requires exact-version approval and distinct, provider-backed delivery truth for buyer distribution.

**How to apply:** Preserve these distinctions in recipient records, provider callbacks, and UI labels. Do not infer sends, delivery, interest, or commitment from copied links, retries, or portal access.

Explicit synthetic-fixture records must remain unqualified even if their fields resemble a fully reviewed profile. Keep their fixture label visible, exclude them from real-buyer pools and demand counts, and do not infer authenticity from completeness.

**Why:** Test/demo records can contain plausible criteria, identity, and funding values but are not real buyer demand.

**How to apply:** Apply the synthetic-fixture marker consistently in qualification status, matching, pool views, and metrics; preserve buyer-reported criteria separately from owner-reviewed evidence.

Buyer-pool coverage must count only current owner-approved buyers with fresh identity, buying-intent, criteria, and funding evidence. Deduct active contract funds and concurrent-deal reservations, and exclude synthetic fixtures from both coverage counts and the market/type grid.

**Why:** Stated buy boxes and funding preferences are not live capacity; active commitments can consume available funds or deal slots, and test fixtures must not make a market look served.

**How to apply:** Keep prospects and stale buyers visible as acquisition/reverification work, but never count them as eligible coverage. Use active transaction reservations when comparing a price band with buyer capacity.
