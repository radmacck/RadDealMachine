name: Executed transaction boundaries
description: Why research refreshes and ordinary conversations must preserve externally executed commitments
---

Supplemental model reasoning is not a change to factual underwriting inputs and must not erase otherwise-current financial research or owner-approved terms.

**Why:** The user requires hypothesis/fact separation and preservation of the verified financial and approval foundation; repeatedly invalidating that foundation merely for new questions defeats the operating workflow.

**How to apply:** Append hypotheses with provenance; keep any real change to evidence or financial inputs subject to the existing freshness and reapproval gates.

An executed agreement and an accepted buyer commitment are historical business events, not classifications derived from current research. Preserve their exact terms and lifecycle when research changes or ordinary coordination is logged.

**Why:** New information can change today's assessment but cannot undo external legal commitments or imply that a call cancelled a signed agreement.

**How to apply:** Use evidenced owner-reviewed cancellation or amendments for changes to executed commitments. Pre-execution paperwork preparation must not itself invalidate the exact approval it is meant to implement.
